## Purpose

Do not ask which method is universally best.

Ask:

```text
Best method for what job, object, phase, risk, proof layer, output need, and stop line?
```

This candidate prevents one flat ranking from becoming false authority. The lower-cause lab shows that the best live-incident methods are not identical to the best rule-build methods.

```text
All methods in map.
Few methods in hand.

One output closes.
```
