## Promotion Rule

No card enters an active selector, active method deck, active guide, helper gate, watcher, automation, or tool surface until it has:

- exact job;
- object / phase / risk / proof-layer fit;
- DoesNotProve line;
- bad-use warning;
- at least one failure case;
- at least one good-use case;
- duplication check;
- adopt / adapt / park / reject / test decision.
