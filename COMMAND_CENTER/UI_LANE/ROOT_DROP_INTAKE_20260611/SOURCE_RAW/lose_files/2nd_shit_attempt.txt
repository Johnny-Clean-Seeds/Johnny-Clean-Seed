A keyword search sees the word `Saturn`.

`LEVEL 0 — ABSENT`

The gate is neither named nor implied.

`LEVEL 1 — NAMED`

The gate name appears, but no job is done.

No proof burden. No blocked claim. Weak.

`LEVEL 2 — IMPLIED`

The gate name is missing, but its job appears.

`LEVEL 3 — APPLIED_WEAK`

Some job appears, but incomplete.

`Proof required.`

Missing exact proof target and unlock condition.

The gate does its own job, stays in lane, names blocked claims, names next gate, and names proof need.

`LEVEL 5 — APPLIED_SHADOW`

The gate appears but abuses its job.

Saturn blocks forever with no unlock condition. Jupiter promotes too fast. Pluto root-dives one weak signal.

`LEVEL 6 — APPLIED_OVERREACH`

The gate decides outside its authority.

Example:

Mercury defines words and then claims proof. Earth confirms file exists and then claims idea is true.

That level system is the missing sharp edge.
