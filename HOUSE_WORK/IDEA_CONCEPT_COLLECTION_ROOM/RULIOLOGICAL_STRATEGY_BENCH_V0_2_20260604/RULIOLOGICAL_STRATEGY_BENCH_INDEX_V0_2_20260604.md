## Coverage And Anti-Gaming

Pairwise fixture coverage is a floor, not a ceiling. Serious rules need pairwise-plus-scars, stateful sequence coverage, holdouts, and Hall-of-Fame replay.

Anti-gaming controls:

- Goodhart guard: a metric that becomes the target stops being enough.
- Specification gaming guard: the literal checklist and intended house outcome must both survive.
- Holdout guard: fixture variants must exist outside shaping.
- Fake telemetry guard: panels and receipts must bind to object, hash, state, and command context.
- Refusal guard: safe-but-useless is not a winning strategy.
- Bloat guard: more source or output is not proof of depth.
- Authority leak guard: winner, proved, elite, saved, and rule language must stay below promotion gates.
